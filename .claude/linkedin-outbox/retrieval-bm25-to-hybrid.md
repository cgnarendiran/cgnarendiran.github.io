Half the time I search for something, I don't know what it's called.

The black rubber ring round your washing machine door goes hard. Water ends up on your floor. You search "black ring washing machine door" and get nothing. The shop calls it a door gasket. You said ring, it said gasket.

So how do you look up a thing you can't name?

Picture a parts shop with forty thousand drawers. There is a catalogue sorted by word, and a man who has worked there thirty years. He hears "black ring, door, water on the floor" and walks to a drawer without looking anything up. Ten thousand customers described things badly to him, and he learned where things live.

Semantic search is that man. It matches what you meant, not the words you typed.

Move him to a trade he never worked. Behind a pharmacy counter he still walks confidently to a drawer. He walks to the wrong one. The catalogue wins there (it never pretended to understand anything).
