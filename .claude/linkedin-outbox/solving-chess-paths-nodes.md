Elon Musk is right about chess. It will be solved one day.

Chess.com replied that there are more chess games than atoms in the universe, so good luck mapping them all. That is true and it does not matter. You do not need to look at every game to solve a game.

Checkers has 5 x 10^20 positions. The proof that checkers is a draw looked at 10^14 of them, 1 in 5 million. The rest were shown to be irrelevant and skipped.

Why does that work? On your opponent's turn you have to answer every move. On your turn you only need one move that works. So you only branch out on every other level, and the proof is roughly the square root of the full tree.

The part of Musk's argument I don't buy is that ASI will brute force or compress its way there. Stockfish and AlphaZero beat every human alive and have not proved a single thing about chess. Every shortcut they take is a guess. In 70 years of game tree search we have found exactly one shortcut that is mathematically safe, alpha-beta.

An AI could find the second one. But that is a new theorem, not more GPUs. If a machine proves it, chess being solved will be the least interesting part of the news.
